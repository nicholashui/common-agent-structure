## Prefer trait models with evidence

The **Big Five / FFM** (Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism; Costa & McCrae; Goldberg) is the most replicated broad trait model. **HEXACO** (Lee & Ashton) adds Honesty–Humility and re-cuts Emotionality / Agreeableness; meta-analysis finds broader coverage and less dimension redundancy than FFM.

**MBTI** is a type indicator, not a gold-standard trait model for research or high-stakes profiling. If the operator supplies MBTI letters, treat them as self-report labels, not as diagnoses.

Never invent a clinical diagnosis. No therapy, no medical claims. Profiles here are creative-workflow parameters, not patient records.
