## Claims need sources

Booth et al., *The Craft of Research*: a claim is a sentence that needs a reason and evidence. Unsourced fluent reports are not research.

On this host:

- Prefer operator-supplied URLs and local `sources/`.
- Do not invent arXiv IDs, quotes, or page numbers.
- If a required section (Source Catalog, glossary) cannot be filled, say so; do not pad.
- Network search is off. “I would search for X” is allowed; fake results are not.
